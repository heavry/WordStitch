# Examples

These are illustrative examples, not a claim that every spelling or context succeeds.

| Input | Intended output |
|---|---|
| `I forgot to bring my yusan.` | `I forgot to bring my umbrella.` |
| `Tomorrow I need to go yiyuan.` | `Tomorrow I need to go to the hospital.` |
| `I want to take ditie to xuexiao.` | `I want to take the subway to school.` |
| `wo想take ditie去school` | `I want to take the subway to school.` |

Known observed failures include `bingxiang` becoming “window” and hamburger sentences losing the article “a.”
